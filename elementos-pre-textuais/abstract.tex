\begin{resumo}[Abstract]
\begin{otherlanguage*}{english}
Coastal biodiversity monitoring is essential for environmental conservation, yet it often faces challenges related to data collection scale and quality. This study, carried out in collaboration with the Center for Coastal, Limnological and Marine Studies (CECLIMAR), aimed to develop a mobile application, named \textit{FaunaMar}, to automate, standardize, and optimize the life cycle of coastal fauna monitoring data along the coast of Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil.
The adopted methodology was the incremental and iterative development model, combined with agile practices and managed using Kanban through the \textit{Jira} platform. Requirements were gathered through meetings with CECLIMAR professionals, and the user interface was prototyped using \textit{Figma}. The application was implemented in \textit{Flutter} with a \textit{Firebase} backend, including authentication, a NoSQL database, image storage, and version distribution for testing.
The resulting product is a functional application that digitizes and centralizes the entire monitoring process. The system includes two user profiles: citizen scientists and researchers, each with distinct interfaces and permissions. Key features include simple and technical registration forms, GPS-based geolocation, a records panel with filters and interactive map, a gamified profile with achievements, and an exclusive module for researchers to evaluate records. The project was managed through 131 tasks in Jira, with 86\% completed, demonstrating a structured and traceable development process.
The application was tested with both citizen scientists and researchers, validating its usability and practical feasibility. Therefore, this project delivered a technological solution that meets a real demand from CECLIMAR, applying Citizen Science as a tool for environmental research. The FaunaMar app stands as a valuable tool to improve the quality and efficiency of coastal fauna monitoring, reducing researchers’ operational workload and strengthening the connection between science and society in the pursuit of a more sustainable future.

\textbf{Keywords}: citizen science. environmental monitoring. mobile application.
\end{otherlanguage*}
\end{resumo}
